The six bug classes we look for

  1. Look-ahead - a statistic computed over a window that includes bars after the entry. Detected by shifting every bar after the signal bar and re-running the strategy. If the decision changes, the strategy read data it could not have had.
  2. Data provenance - duplicate timestamps, merged markets, gaps, and spreads that are constants pretending to be regimes.
  3. Unit of analysis - 31,579 trades from 268 weekly events has an n of 268, not 31,579.
  4. Units - the broker quotes swap per lot; the cost model consumes it per unit. A 100,000x error that runs without complaint.
  5. The fill model - who pays the spread and when. We found our own simulator charging the spread twice on a long entry and not at all on a short exit. In the pre-fix model an identical long and short paid different amounts on the same bars, which means the random-direction control every "beats the baseline" claim depends on was not a control at all.
  6. Position count - a mirror with no cap on simultaneous positions is testing a portfolio, not an account. Every EA we ship has an InpMaxOpen input; the simulator now has the same parameter, and every dossier reports the peak number of positions open at once.

None of these raise an exception. The backtest runs, prints a number, and the number is wrong.
