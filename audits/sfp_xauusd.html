Method

Walk-forward evaluation on 31336 bars of XAUUSDm H1 from the broker's own feed. Parameters are chosen on a fixed grid using only data that existed before each test window; only out-of-sample numbers are reported. Every signal is paired with a coin-flipped control that keeps the entry bar, the stop distance and the target distance identical, so the only thing that changes is direction. Costs are the real ones: per-bar spread from the feed, slippage on every fill, and overnight swap converted from per-lot to per-unit.

478 of the 31814 rows in this file are not H1 bars at all - they are bars spaced 24x apart. MT5 silently returns higher-timeframe bars for the period where the requested timeframe does not exist. This lab trims them before running anything. An EA written by hand usually does not, and a strategy backtested on those rows is being evaluated on a timeframe it does not use.

6.2 Look-ahead

For each signal we shift every bar strictly after the signal bar by a constant offset, keeping OHLC shape intact. If the decision changes - the signal disappears, or flips direction - the strategy read data that did not exist yet.

CLEAN. 1382 signals in the series; the 25 sampled were re-evaluated after shifting every bar up to 12 bars after their signal bar, with no change in existence or direction.

6.3 Unit of analysis

1382 trades came from 583 independent decisions (2.4 trades per decision). Counting trades as observations inflates the sample size and narrows the confidence interval artificially.

9. What this means for you

The difference between this system and a coin flip is not statistically distinguishable. Whatever the vendor's screenshot shows, on your broker's own data, with your costs, the direction this system picks is not better than chance.

Two things follow. First, any profit you have seen from this system is explainable by luck, by the parameters chosen after the fact, or by a backtest that read the future. Second, the money is not in the signal; it is in the cost structure, and that is measurable.
