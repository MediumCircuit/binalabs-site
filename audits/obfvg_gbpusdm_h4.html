Audit: obfvg on GBPUSDm H4

Date: 2026-11-05
Auditor: Bina Labs
Verdict: NO EDGE

- edge -0.5577, CI [-1.3003, 0.2524] includes zero - direction is indistinguishable from chance
- 31 out-of-sample trades
2 of 5 kill criteria triggered.

Method

Walk-forward evaluation on 8497 bars of GBPUSDm H4 from the broker's own feed. Parameters are chosen on a fixed grid using only data that existed before each test window; only out-of-sample numbers are reported. Every signal is paired with a coin-flipped control that keeps the entry bar, the stop distance and the target distance identical, so the only thing that changes is direction. Costs are the real ones: per-bar spread from the feed, slippage on every fill, and overnight swap converted from per-lot to per-unit.

Out-of-sample expectancy 0.9414 R per trade (95% CI [0.2930, 1.5890]) over 31 trades. No edge by the criteria below.

6.2 Look-ahead

For each signal we shift every bar strictly after the signal bar by a constant offset, keeping OHLC shape intact. If the decision changes - the signal disappears, or flips direction - the strategy read data that did not exist yet.

CLEAN. 28 signals in the series; the 28 sampled were re-evaluated after shifting every bar up to 12 bars after their signal bar, with no change in existence or direction.

6.3 Unit of analysis

28 trades came from 28 independent decisions (1.0 trades per decision). Counting trades as observations inflates the sample size and narrows the confidence interval artificially.

6.5 Simultaneous positions

The mirror held up to 1 position(s) at once across the 28 closed trades it booked. Every EA shipped from this lab has an InpMaxOpen input (OB_FVG_EA.mq5: 1), and the simulator now supports the same cap. A strategy that fires repeatedly while a position is open is not trading the account the terminal would have run on - it is trading a portfolio.

This run was executed uncapped. If your EA has InpMaxOpen set (they all do), the peak above is the number you need to compare with it.

Run again with InpMaxOpen = 1 and the identical signal set: 28 trades instead of 28 (0 could never have been placed), expectancy -0.3577 R per trade instead of -0.3577 R. The confidence interval on 28 trades is not a confidence interval on the 28 trades an account could actually hold.

Signals generated: 28. Trades actually closed: 28. Orders that never filled: 0. Trades rejected because a position was already open: 0. The last two numbers are the difference between a strategy and a strategy that can be executed.

9. What this means for you

The difference between this system and a coin flip is not statistically distinguishable. Whatever the vendor's screenshot shows, on your broker's own data, with your costs, the direction this system picks is not better than chance.

Two things follow. First, any profit you have seen from this system is explainable by luck, by the parameters chosen after the fact, or by a backtest that read the future. Second, the money is not in the signal; it is in the cost structure, and that is measurable.
