EA audit service

You send the EA. We run it through the harness. You get a document with the verdict, the evidence, and the exact commands so you can re-run it yourself.

What is in the dossier

  1. Verdict in one line, with the confidence interval
  2. Kill criteria, written down before the run
  3. Walk-forward table - every run, not just the good one
  4. Random-direction baseline - same trades, directions coin-flipped
  5. Cost stress - same signals, spread doubled, one extra pip of slippage
  6. Integrity audit - provenance, look-ahead, unit of analysis, swap units, the bid/ask fill model, and whether the trades the backtest booked could actually have been placed on an account with a position cap
  7. Failure list - every parameter set that lost money
  8. Reproducibility appendix - the exact commands

What we do not do

  • We do not "improve" the EA or re-tune it to turn a losing system into a winning one
  • We do not give trading advice
  • We do not take custody of your account
  • We do not sell you a replacement EA as a result of an audit

Why the verdict is believable

Sixteen bugs in our own testing infrastructure were found by the same checks we run on your EA. Four of them changed a verdict. A session mean computed over bars that had not happened yet moved a strategy from +0.1497 R to -0.0483 R. A CFTC feed that merged three gold markets created 168 duplicate dates and a phantom edge. A swap quoted per lot consumed per unit produced -616 R per trade. None of these raise an error.

The credential is not that we are good at finding bugs. It is that we found them in our own code, published them, and fixed them.

Intake

Send: the EA file (source strongly preferred), the exact parameter set you run, the broker and account type, the symbols and timeframes, the date range, and the tester model used. Spread and slippage decide whether an edge is real, so those are not optional.
